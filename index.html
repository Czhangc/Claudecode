Coping Strategies

Deep Breathing

Breathe in for 4 counts, hold for 4, exhale for 4, hold for 4. Repeat 4 times.

5-4-3-2-1 Grounding

Name 5 things you see, 4 you can touch, 3 you hear, 2 you smell, and 1 you taste.

Progressive Muscle Relaxation

Tense and release each muscle group, starting from your toes to your head.

Mindful Walking

Take a slow walk, focusing on each step and your breath. Notice your surroundings.

Finding Professional Help

While this AI companion can provide support, professional therapy offers personalized care for your specific needs. Consider reaching out to:

  • Licensed therapists and counselors
  • Psychiatrists for medication management
  • Support groups for shared experiences
  • Your primary care physician for referrals

Many therapists offer sliding scale fees or accept insurance. Online therapy platforms like BetterHelp and Talkspace can also be accessible options.
